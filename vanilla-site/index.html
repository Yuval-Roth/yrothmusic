About

Hi, I'm Yiftach.

Making music is my passion.

Every musical piece has a story behind it. While there are many ways to tell a story, the right storyteller will let you drift through the atmosphere and emphasize your feelings towards it.

How did I get here?

I started my musical life as an electric guitar player. As the years went by, I learned to play the bass, drums and keyboard. Around the age of 22 I was finally certain that making music is my purpose and desire, so I enrolled in music production school.

Nowadays I produce and teach music. I am also a member and producer of the band Fox Temple.

For me, one of the most interesting aspects of music production is sound design and mixing, and I put a lot of time and effort in creating the right sound for every piece. I love sharing my knowledge & skills with others, and welcome you to come work with me.
